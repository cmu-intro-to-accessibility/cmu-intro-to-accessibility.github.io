### Design Vision Projects

In this milestone, you will begin designing your future vision of this accessibility technology. Collect all of your analyses and ideas from P4 and turn them into low-fi scripts, sketches, and storyboards for the 3-minute video you are creating. 

You have full creative freedom on the length and content of your scenes. In an envisioning video, you want to *show* what your vision is, not just talk about it out loud. Create characters with spoken dialogue who live through the future you envision. Using Courier 12-point font, your draft script should be about 3 pages long. This conforms to the TV industry standard of one minute of screen time per page. 

Put all of your materials into Canva, Figma, or other creative cloud tools of your choice. Share these with the course instructors. 

Record a low-fi, table-read version of your video. Act out the scenes (from your seat at the table) with paper props. Overlay a spoken narration. Don't spend any time on choreography, blocking, costumes, scenery, graphics, technology, or anything that would take effort. The goal in this milestone isn't to produce a slick video; it's to show the gist of the story you want to tell. Upload your video to YouTube and mark it as unlisted.

Before finalizing the concept, you must show your low-fi video to 2 people with the target disability to obtain their feedback. These may be the same people you invited to your workshop in P4. Do they like the idea? Do they resonate with the characters? Do they want this future to come to pass? Ask what they'd like you to change or improve to better realize their vision. If they consent, audio and video record the interviews. Be sure to take notes as well.

As per the IRB protocol you submitted with P3, you must prepare recruitment flyers, consent forms, and questionnaires. If these interviewees were not in your co-design workshops, be sure to have them fill out and sign consent forms. 

For your deliverables, turn in your scripts, sketches, storyboards, Canva, Figma, and/or creative cloud document URLs, YouTube video URL, interview notes, and interview transcripts.
